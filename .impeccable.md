# Design Context

## Users
Students in a Swedish electronics-assembly (montör) training course, learning to solder by hand. They open the
site on their phones at the bench or at home, often after a teacher shows it in class. The teacher may also
put it on a projector. Swedish is the main language.

## Use cases
- Practise the timing of a through-hole joint: preheat, feed solder, remove solder, remove iron.
- See what goes wrong and why: cold joint, burnt flux, lifted pad, too little or too much solder.
- Learn the theory around it: tips, leaded vs lead-free solder, through-hole vs surface mount, safety.

## Brand personality
A workshop service manual: practical, precise, a little blunt. Feels like a technical drawing and an
inspection stamp, not a mobile game with confetti. Light paper background, ink text, copper accent.

## Design decisions
- Fonts: Big Shoulders Display (headings, labels, buttons) + Atkinson Hyperlegible (body, chosen for readability).
- Palette in OKLCH: warm paper, blue-black ink, copper accent. Status colours for good / warn / bad / cold.
- The board view is a cross-section drawing on grid paper with callout labels.
- Hold buttons look like physical keys with a solid offset shadow that disappears when pressed.
- Results appear in place of the controls with a rotated GODKÄND / UNDERKÄND stamp. No modals.
